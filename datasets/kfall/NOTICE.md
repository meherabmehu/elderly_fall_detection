# KFall — vendored copy with terms notice

**Contents:** the public Kaggle mirror
[`usmanabbasi2002/kfall-dataset`](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/usmanabbasi2002/kfall-dataset)
of the KFall dataset: `sensor_data/SAxxTxxRxx.csv` (100 Hz low-back IMU,
6 channels) + `label_data/SAxx_label.xlsx` (video-grounded onset/impact
frames) + `KFall_Dataset_Directory.txt`. 5,075 trials, 32 subjects.

**Citation obligation:** Yu X, Jang J, Xiong S. *A Large-Scale Open Motion
Dataset (KFall) and Benchmark Algorithms for Detecting Pre-impact Fall of the
Elderly Using Wearable Inertial Sensors.* Front. Aging Neurosci. 2021.
BibTeX: `paper_support/refs.bib`.

**Terms notice you should read before redistributing further:** the official
KFall page (https://sites.google.com/view/kfalldataset/home) asks users to
register and states that the dataset should not be transferred to third
parties. This copy serves this project's research reproducibility (the
mirror itself is publicly available without registration) — if you reuse it,
cite Yu et al. 2021 and respect the upstream terms.

Canonical upstream: video-synchronised labels derived at the University of
South Carolina; 100 Hz; low-back (L5) inertial placement — the primary
pre-impact label source in this work.
